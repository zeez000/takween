# Requirements

## Business

- Investment ceiling INR 30,000; availability 3-4 hours daily; Windows laptop.
- Custom plastic FDM printing with PLA and PETG.
- Engineering students, startups, manufacturers, local businesses and individuals.
- Initial profit goal INR 5,000-10,000 monthly, a target rather than a forecast.
- Planned office inside Fateen Technologies, Rajajinagar, Bengaluru.
- Expansion possibilities: recycling, outsourced metal manufacturing, more printers.

Confirmed brand: TAKWEEN. Tagline: YOU NAME IT. WE MAKE IT.

Statement: Born from Fateen. Built for what's next.

configurable and unconfirmed; no booking claims until confirmed.

## Website acceptance

Homepage and animated hero; about; printing explanation; custom services;
categories and honest empty portfolio; STL/3MF/STEP selection; enquiry draft;
materials/capabilities; configurable WhatsApp; Fateen; responsive footer.
Local selection is explicitly not a secure upload. Draft generation is explicitly
not a submitted enquiry. Features deferred to backend have clear placeholders.
Desktop and iPhone layouts, touch navigation, keyboard focus, 16px form inputs,
reduced-motion support and no horizontal overflow at 320-1440px.

## Future backend

Customer enquiry management, secure private design-file uploads, manual quotes,
cost calculator, authorised order tracking, filament inventory, product catalogue,
profit/expense reporting and administrative authentication.
A full business website and operating platform, never calculator-only.

No public deployment or purchased services without owner approval.
