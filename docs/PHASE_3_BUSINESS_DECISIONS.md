# Pending owner decisions before hosted business activation

No item below is an approved policy. The software's technical bounds and synthetic
test values are not commercial rates, legal terms or promises of service.

| Decision | Owner confirmation needed | Current boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TAKWEEN LABS contracting entity | Exact legal/person/entity name, approved business identification and who issues the quotation/invoice. | Entity is entered manually; no invented registration or identity. |
| GST / tax treatment | Applicability, registration details if any, tax classification/breakdown and rounding/invoice requirements with an appropriate adviser. | One manual aggregate tax percentage; blank blocks approval. Zero requires an explicit entry. Quotation PDF is not a tax invoice. |
| Terms and validity | Approved quotation terms, validity, revisions, cancellation/refund/delivery responsibilities and any guarantees the owner can actually support. | Blank terms/entity/validity block approval; no default commercial promises. |
| Customer acceptance evidence | Accepted communication channels, evidence to retain, who may record acceptance/rejection, identity verification and handling disputed/late responses. | Owner records a bounded evidence note. No customer acceptance link, signature or payment proof is assumed. |
| Fateen authority and referral | Approved contracting/routing authority, recipient/contact, scope, legal relationship and consent language. | Separate machining category and explicit referral consent; no automatic sharing or claim of Fateen authority. |
| Retention and backups | Approved retention/deletion schedule, access/audit retention, backup location/frequency, restore drills and handling of downloaded PDFs. | No automatic deletion. No production backup guarantee; private storage/restore procedures require approval. |
| Completed/cancelled order corrections | Who may amend costs or reopen jobs, correction evidence, replacement orders and accounting/audit treatment. | Terminal orders cannot transition or append production observations. Existing history is not rewritten. |
| Owner MFA recovery | Recovery custodian/process, identity checks, lost-device handling, emergency access review and session revocation. | Invitation-only allowlist + AAL2. No public reset/elevation bypass; hosted recovery procedure unapproved. |

Any future change must preserve audit history and require explicit owner review.
Real email, hosted migrations/deployment, customer uploads, payments and indexing
remain separately gated. No pending decision is silently filled by this checklist.
