# TAKWEEN UX and business review

The public copy identifies custom plastic 3D printing, PLA/PETG, Rajajinagar/Bengaluru and preparing-to-launch status. This is enough information to explain the intended business, but no five-second customer study was conducted. Do not claim validated comprehension or conversion.

The enquiry form explicitly prepares a local draft, says nothing is sent, and instructs customers to paste it into their email client. Browser regression confirmed the official mailto address and no mutation requests during draft preparation. A mailto link cannot guarantee an email client exists or that delivery succeeds. Show the copyable address as a fallback, after approval if needed.

Admin and tracking are public explanatory outlines, not security-protected operational dashboards. They currently expose no customer records in inspected source. Do not label them production-ready. No pricing, build volume or turnaround guarantee should be invented. Current configuration marks build volume/layer heights to be confirmed and turnaround agreed per quotation. Fateen capabilities remain unconfirmed, which is appropriate.

The file selector is local only, but its 25 MB wording is technically MiB and diverges from the approved future upload contract. This is U01, not an upload exploit. Customers may expect file selection to attach their design to email, so the existing not-uploaded/no-attachment language must remain prominent.

Privacy and commercial-use expectations need an owner-approved policy before accepting customer designs. Cover handling of confidential CAD, intellectual-property authorization, third-party email handling, retention/deletion, access and prohibited unsafe or infringing orders. No legal compliance determination was made. Do not promise encryption, certifications or retention periods without confirming actual implementation.

The hero remains unapproved. Desktop actual-size capture shows prominent depth and dark walls. Proposed design review should first retain exact reference outlines and neutral front-facing pose, then reduce depth/contrast if necessary. Arbitrary gap widening risks changing the brand. Default view, bounded side views and actual mobile rendering all need owner approval.

Accessibility checks in this pass were limited to accessible-name-based Playwright interactions, reduced motion and no-JavaScript readability. Not a WCAG certification. Manual screen-reader, contrast, full focus-order, zoom and touch-target audits remain outstanding.

Evidence is in artifacts/red-team. No frontend or wording changes made.
